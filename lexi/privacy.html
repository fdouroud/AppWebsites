Lexi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Lexi

Summary

Lexi does not collect, sell or share your data. It has no server of its own, no ads and no tracking. Your cards and progress stay on your own devices and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive.

Data in the app

What you put in: your flashcards (words, translations, examples, your own notes), your review progress, daily counts and settings. They are stored only on the phone or computer where you use Lexi.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Lexi asks for access only to the files it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It uses this access only to:

  • keep a copy of your cards, progress and settings in a folder named “Lexi” in your Drive, so your phone and your computer stay in step;
  • keep the sound files of premium voices there, so each word's sound is fetched only once.

Lexi cannot see your other Drive files. Data travels directly between your device and Google Drive; it never passes through any server of ours, and we have no access to it.

Lexi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Premium voices, examples and translations (optional)

If you enter your own Google API key, Lexi sends the text of a card (the word, its meaning or an example sentence) directly from your device to Google, only when you use these features:

  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech or Gemini, to read the text aloud;
  • Gemini, to write an example sentence, translate an example or explain a word.

These requests are made with your key, under your own agreement with Google; Google's terms and privacy policy apply to them. Without a key, nothing is sent and Lexi uses the device's own voices. The key is stored only on the device where you entered it.

Permissions

Lexi uses the internet only for the optional features above. It does not use your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, sold or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Lexi (or clearing its data) removes everything it stored on that device.
  • Delete the “Lexi” folder in your Google Drive to remove the synced copy and sound files.
  • You can remove Lexi's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Lexi does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
