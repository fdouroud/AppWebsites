Lexi

Flashcards for the words you meet while reading

What Lexi does

Lexi is a personal vocabulary app for Android phones and the Mac. It shows one word at a time: swipe right if you know it, left if you don't. Words you don't know come back a few cards later, and words you know come back after growing gaps, so they stay in your memory.

  • Spaced repetition, your way: set your own steps (for example 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 … days), or let the smart mode (FSRS) work out the best time for each word.
  • Learning methods: both directions, fill-the-gap sentences, listening cards, and a “Hard words” list with your own notes.
  • Read-aloud of every word and example, with the device's own voices or premium Google voices.
  • Examples and translations written by Gemini on request, clearly marked.
  • Sync between your phone and your Mac through your own Google Drive.
  • Decks per language, daily goal and streak, progress charts.

Your data

Lexi has no server, no account of its own, no ads and no tracking. Your cards stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in a “Lexi” folder in your own Google Drive. See the privacy policy.
